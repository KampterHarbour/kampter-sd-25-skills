- Standard generation duration and resolution range

- Video-extension source, extension, and final-length range

- Prompt organization order

## What belongs to this Skill

Do not attribute the following to ByteDance as hard product rules:

- Quick co-creation, director collaboration, and diagnosis routing
- Three-question turn limit for beginner dialogue
- Creative brief format, delivery format, templates, examples, and quality gate
- Advice on shot density, continuity anchors, wording, or negative-constraint length

## Citation rules

- Cite only facts explicitly listed above as source-backed.
- When the source does not state a detail, say “the current manual does not specify this” rather than infer it.
- Do not describe this manual as Seedance API documentation.
- Do not claim ByteDance affiliation, endorsement, or ownership of this independent Skill.
